Pricing & Rates

Understand how shipping costs are estimated in RouteWise. Our pricing is based on transport mode, shipment weight, and route structure.

What affects your shipping cost?

Transport mode

Different transport types have different costs. Air is usually faster but more expensive. Sea is slower but more cost-efficient for heavy shipments.

Shipment weight

Heavier shipments increase total cost. Some transport modes become more efficient for larger weights.

Route structure

Routes with more stops or transfers may cost more. Direct routes are usually simpler and sometimes cheaper.

Minimum charges

Each route has a minimum cost. Even small shipments will not go below this base price.

Rate guide by transport mode

Sea freight 🚢

  • Base cost: ¥4,000 – ¥7,500
  • Cost per kg: ¥0.6 – ¥1.0
  • Best for heavy and bulk shipments
  • Slower delivery time

Air freight ✈️

  • Base cost: ¥1,100 – ¥2,000
  • Cost per kg: ¥28 – ¥55
  • Fastest delivery option
  • Higher cost

Road transport 🚚

  • Base cost: ¥800 – ¥1,200
  • Cost per kg: ¥2 – ¥3.5
  • Used for inland delivery
  • Medium speed and cost

Rail transport 🚆

  • Base cost: ¥1,700 – ¥1,800
  • Cost per kg: ~¥3
  • Stable and reliable option
  • Balanced between cost and speed

How is the total cost calculated?

Estimated cost = Base cost + (Cost per kg × Shipment weight)

If the calculated cost is lower than the minimum charge, the minimum charge will be used instead.

Base cost
Fixed starting cost for the route
Cost per kg
Price per kilogram
Shipment weight
Total shipment weight

Example

  • Shipment weight: 100 kg
  • Base cost: ¥1,500
  • Cost per kg: ¥30

Estimated cost = ¥1,500 + (¥30 × 100) = ¥4,500

Why do different routes have different prices?

  • Faster routes usually cost more
  • Air transport increases cost
  • Multi-leg routes may include additional charges
  • Heavier shipments may change the best route

Important

All prices shown in RouteWise are estimates. Actual costs may vary depending on route conditions and logistics providers.
