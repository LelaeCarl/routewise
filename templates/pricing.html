Pricing & rates

How RouteWise estimates cost from mode, weight, and route shape. Figures are illustrative bands for the demo network.

What drives cost

Transport mode

Air is faster and priced higher per kg; sea is slower and often better for heavy freight.

Shipment weight

Total cost scales with kilograms. Some modes improve at higher weights.

Route structure

More legs or transfers can add cost versus a simpler path.

Minimum charges

Each leg can enforce a floor—small shipments still pay a base amount.

Mode rate bands

Indicative ranges used in the model—not live carrier quotes.

Sea

  • Base: ¥4,000 – ¥7,500
  • Per kg: ¥0.6 – ¥1.0
  • Heavy / bulk; slower transit.

Air

  • Base: ¥1,100 – ¥2,000
  • Per kg: ¥28 – ¥55
  • Fastest option; higher per kg.

Road

  • Base: ¥800 – ¥1,200
  • Per kg: ¥2 – ¥3.5
  • Inland legs; mid speed and cost.

Rail

  • Base: ¥1,700 – ¥1,800
  • Per kg: ~¥3
  • Stable; balanced cost vs time.

Cost formula

Per leg (simplified)

Estimated cost = Base + (Cost per kg × Weight)

If that value is below the mode minimum, the minimum applies.

Base
Fixed charge for the segment.
Per kg
Variable part by weight.
Weight
Shipment mass (or chargeable for air).

Example

  • Weight: 100 kg
  • Base: ¥1,500
  • Per kg: ¥30

= ¥1,500 + (¥30 × 100) = ¥4,500

Why routes differ in price

  • Faster legs often cost more.
  • Air segments raise the total.
  • More transfers can add fees.
  • Heavier cargo can change the best path.

Disclaimer

All figures are estimates for planning. Real invoices depend on carriers, fuel, and lane conditions.
