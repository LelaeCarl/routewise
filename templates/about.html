About RouteWise

RouteWise is decision support for cross-border logistics between China and Kenya. It models multi-modal corridors as a weighted graph and uses shortest-path optimization to recommend routes that balance cost, transit time, and operational clarity.

Problem

Cross-border trade involves sea, air, rail, and road legs that interact in non-obvious ways. A cheaper route may take much longer; a faster route may cost disproportionately more. RouteWise surfaces these trade-offs so operators can compare structured recommendations instead of relying on ad hoc estimates.

Multi-modal handoffs

Ports, airports, ICDs, and inland hubs across the corridor.

Cost vs time

Objectives produce different optimal paths on the same network.

Non-obvious routing

Graph output is turned into leg-level explanations and KPIs.

Transparency

Rationale, comparison across objectives, and sensitivity where applicable.

Capabilities

  • Route planning — direction, endpoints, cargo weight, objective.
  • Multi-modal paths — sea, air, rail, road segments.
  • Objective comparison — lowest cost, fastest delivery, and balanced tradeoff side by side.
  • Structured results — KPIs, legs table, path narrative.

Stack

Backend Python, Flask
Routing Dijkstra
UI Jinja, Tailwind
Data JSON network

Model

Directed graph: nodes are facilities; edges have cost and time. Edge weights for search depend on the selected objective and shipment weight.

Context

Academic project: the UI is intended to expose assumptions and trade-offs for evaluation of routing behaviour.
